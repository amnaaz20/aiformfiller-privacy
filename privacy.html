Privacy Policy for AIFormFiller

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

AIFormFiller ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the AIFormFiller application.

1. Information We Process

AIFormFiller does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

The App allows users to select or capture an image of a form. The image may contain information entered on the form by the user.

2. Form Images

When a user submits a form image to the AI-powered functionality, the image may be transmitted to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Gemini is used to identify form fields and provide information about what may need to be entered in those fields.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive, confidential, financial, medical, or other highly private information when using the AI-powered feature.

3. Google Gemini API

AIFormFiller uses Google's Gemini API to provide AI-powered image analysis and form assistance.

When the AI feature is used, relevant images and instructions may be transmitted to Google for processing by the Gemini service.

The handling and retention of information processed through Google's Gemini API are subject to Google's applicable terms, policies, and the configuration of the API service being used.

4. Data Storage

AIFormFiller does not intentionally maintain a server-side database containing users' personal information or completed forms.

Temporary processing or storage may occur through third-party services used to provide the application's functionality.

5. Data Sharing

AIFormFiller does not sell users' personal information.

When a user chooses to use the AI-powered form analysis feature, relevant information may be transmitted to Google Gemini for processing.

We do not intentionally share users' information with unrelated third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Account Information

AIFormFiller does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its basic functionality.

7. Advertising and Analytics

AIFormFiller does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, payment services, or other third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. User Control

Users choose whether to select or capture a form image and whether to use the AI-powered analysis functionality.

Users should review the information contained in a form before submitting it to the AI-powered feature.

9. Children's Privacy

AIFormFiller is a general-purpose form assistance application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application's functionality, third-party services, or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

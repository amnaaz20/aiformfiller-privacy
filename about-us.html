About AIFormFiller

AIFormFiller is an AI-powered form assistance application designed to make understanding and completing forms easier.

What is AIFormFiller?

AIFormFiller helps users understand blank or unfamiliar forms. Users can select or capture an image of a form, and the application uses AI-powered analysis to identify form fields and explain what information may need to be entered.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make form filling simpler, clearer, and more convenient by using modern AI technology to provide helpful explanations about form fields.

AI-Powered Assistance

AIFormFiller uses Google's Gemini API for AI-powered image analysis and form assistance. When a user chooses to use the AI feature, the relevant image and instructions may be processed through Google's Gemini service.

Privacy

We respect user privacy and aim to minimize unnecessary data collection. For information about how information is handled, please review our Privacy Policy.
